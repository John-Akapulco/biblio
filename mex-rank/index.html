MEX candidates ranked by ΔEhull

The 266 candidates of the mex/ campaign (MCP project), relaxed with the MACE-MP universal potential and ranked by their energy above (or below) the hull of their competing phases, using the Materials Project GGA/GGA+U (MP2020) hull as reference. Machine-learning pre-screening: a ranking for the DFT queue, not a stability verdict. Generated October 2, 2026; same list as Table 3 (appendix A) of the accompanying report.

Accuracy against DFT on the 13 Materials Project ground states among the candidates: MACE 11 meV/atom, CHGNet 61 meV/atom (mean absolute error). Not reliable for nitrides (X = N) and E = C (caveat column).

ΔEhull (meV/atom): E(candidate, MLIP, MP2020-corrected) − E(MP DFT hull of the competing phases, excluding every MEX polymorph), at the MEX composition; negative = below the hull. CHGNet ✓: first quartile of the CHGNet ranking among candidates without caveat. DFT (MP): Materials Project value of the ground state of the composition, when it matches the candidate. Priority (no caveat): 1 if ΔEhull < 0, 2 if 0–50 meV/atom. a, c: conventional cell of the MACE-relaxed structure (Å). Bold compound: absent from Materials Project.

Data: ranking.csv.
